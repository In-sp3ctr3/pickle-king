# Share and Results Repair

## Goal

Make every exported image worth sharing, let the user inspect the exact PNG
before it leaves the device, and repair the tournament-results and setup layouts
at phone and iPad sizes.

## Requirements

- Result, recap, and stats exports are fixed 1080×1350 PNGs.
- Full-bracket exports are fixed 1600×1200 PNGs and keep every round in frame.
- Completed brackets show the champion prominently, the final score, a podium,
  winner paths, and faded or struck losers.
- Every share entry point opens a local preview with native Share and explicit
  Download actions. No social-specific integration is added.
- The results screen leads with champion, final opponent, final score, record,
  and differential. Prominent prose must be factual.
- Setup legends, player rows, draw-choice copy, and number suffixes remain
  aligned at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.

## Privacy

Generated images stay on device until the operator chooses Share or Download.
No name, score, or image is uploaded by Pickle King.
