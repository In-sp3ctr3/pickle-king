# Decision Council Record

## Positions tested

- **Contrarian:** random draws do not prevent mismatches; node-level participant
  replacement corrupts history; one giant share image is unreadable.
- **First principles:** identity, draw structure, score history, and presentation
  are separate concerns. Preserve one court, dependency order, and stable IDs.
- **Expansionist:** expose two intentional draw experiences, two replay paths,
  comeback-aware copy, and a focused share sheet.
- **User advocate:** say exactly what each mode trades off; keep the large tap
  gesture but make correction and addition unmistakable.
- **Executor:** reuse the current deterministic bracket and canvas foundations;
  add no backend and no speculative format engine.

## Consensus

Ship Competitive and Social draw styles, earliest-round start flexibility,
global identity rename, exact/new replay, three focused share artifacts, and
event-backed comeback context. Defer round robin because it changes the timing
model and product structure rather than repairing the existing knockout flow.
